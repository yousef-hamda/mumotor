# Product Hunt launch — ready-to-use copy

Product Hunt is a top priority: the launch page becomes a permanent, citeable
backlink + review source that AI assistants scrape. Do it once, properly.

## Prep (start ~2 weeks before)
- Create a maker account; engage genuinely (upvote, comment) to warm it up.
- Line up 10–20 real people who'll try Mumotor and comment on launch day.
- Launch **Tue–Thu at 12:01am PT**. Be available all day to reply to every comment.

## Name / tagline (pick one)
- **Mumotor** — *The website + booking builder for driving instructors*
- **Mumotor** — *A professional site + online booking for driving instructors, in minutes*
- **Mumotor** — *One tool for a driving instructor's website, bookings and students*

## Description (the "what is it")
> Mumotor is an all-in-one, no-code platform built specifically for driving
> instructors and small driving schools. Answer a short wizard and it generates a
> professional website (1 of 12 designs) plus a full back office: online lesson
> booking with double-booking protection, one-time-code student enrollment, a
> student account area, packages & pricing, reviews, chat, and automatic email
> reminders — all from one dashboard. It's trilingual (Hebrew, Arabic, English)
> with full right-to-left support. One simple plan, ₪199/month, cancel anytime.

## First comment (maker's story — post this yourself at launch)
