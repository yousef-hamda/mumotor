# Directory & listing submissions — checklist

Get Mumotor listed everywhere AI assistants scrape for "tools / SaaS / alternatives"
answers. Use the copy block below for consistency. Track status in the table.

## Reusable copy block
- **Name:** Mumotor
- **URL:** https://mumotor.com
- **Category:** Website Builder / Appointment & Booking / Small Business Software
- **Tagline:** The website + booking builder for driving instructors.
- **One-liner:** All-in-one, no-code platform that gives a driving instructor a
  professional website plus online lesson booking, student enrollment, reminders
  and reviews — trilingual (Hebrew/Arabic/English, RTL). ₪199/month, cancel anytime.
- **Description (long):** Mumotor is built specifically for driving instructors and
  small driving schools. A short wizard generates a professional website (1 of 12
  designs) and a full back office: online lesson booking with double-booking
  protection, one-time-code student enrollment, a student account area, packages &
  pricing, reviews, chat and automatic emails — all from one dashboard. Fully
  trilingual with right-to-left support.
- **Pricing:** One plan, ₪199/month, everything included.
- **Keywords:** driving instructor website, driving school website builder,
  driving lesson booking, driving school software, trilingual website builder.
