# What Mumotor Does — The Complete Guide

*A plain-English description of everything Mumotor can do, written so anyone can understand it — no technical knowledge needed. Nothing is left out: every page, button, field, option, message, email, and automatic behaviour is listed.*

---

## What Mumotor is, in one paragraph

**Mumotor is an all-in-one website builder and business tool made specifically for driving instructors.** A driving teacher answers a short set of questions, picks one of 12 ready-made website designs, and Mumotor instantly builds them a professional, bookable website. From that point on, the teacher runs their whole business — student sign-ups, lesson bookings, daily access codes, schedule reports, reminders, reviews, and messaging — from one simple dashboard. Their students get their own area of the website where they enroll, book tomorrow's lesson, chat with their instructor, and leave reviews. Everything works in **three languages — English, Hebrew, and Arabic** — and automatically flips to right-to-left reading for Hebrew and Arabic. All prices are shown in Israeli Shekels (₪).

---

## The three "sides" of Mumotor

1. **The Mumotor platform** — the public marketing website, sign-up/sign-in, the website builder, and the design editor. This is where a teacher discovers Mumotor and creates their site.
2. **The teacher's own website** — the professional, public website Mumotor builds for each instructor, plus the private dashboard the teacher uses to run everything.
3. **The student's area** — the pages on the teacher's website where their students enroll, book lessons, manage their account, and leave reviews.

This guide walks through all three, in order, and finishes with the settings available on each side, the emails Mumotor sends, and the automatic behind-the-scenes behaviour.

---

## A note that applies to the entire product: languages

- Everything — the marketing site, the builder, the dashboard, every teacher website, and every student page — works in **English, Hebrew, and Arabic**.
- Choosing Hebrew or Arabic flips the entire layout to **right-to-left** reading, and arrows/chevrons mirror automatically.
- **Numbers always stay in standard digits (0–9)** in every language — dates, times, prices, phone numbers, and codes never switch to Arabic or Hebrew numerals.
- Each teacher's website has its **own** language (the one they chose when building it), independent of what language a visitor's browser is set to. A student always sees the site in the language the teacher picked.
- Text a teacher personally types (their bio, custom plan names, their own tagline) is shown exactly as they wrote it — Mumotor never machine-translates a teacher's own words. Everything Mumotor generates around it, though, is fully translated.

# PART 2 — Accounts & Signing In

A teacher needs a Mumotor account to publish and manage a site. (Students do **not** use these pages — they have their own separate, simpler sign-in on the teacher's website, covered in Part 7.)

### Create an account (Register)
Fields: **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone** (required — so students and Mumotor can reach the teacher), and **Password** (at least 8 characters, with a show/hide eye button). Before submitting, Mumotor checks the phone looks valid and the password is long enough, showing a friendly message if not. On success, the teacher lands in their dashboard.

### Sign in (Login)
Fields: **Email** and **Password** (with show/hide). A **"Forgot password?"** link is available. On success, the teacher is taken to wherever they were heading, or the dashboard. (In local testing only, demo credentials are pre-filled — never in the real product.)

### Forgot password
Enter your account email; Mumotor sends a reset link (that works once and expires in 30 minutes). For privacy, the confirmation never reveals whether that email actually has an account.

### Reset password
Opened from the emailed link. Enter a new password twice; it must be at least 8 characters and both entries must match. On success you're sent to sign in.

### Verify email
When a teacher registers, Mumotor emails them a verification link. Opening it confirms their email ("Email verified"). If the link is missing or expired, it explains how to request a fresh one. Until verified, a gentle (dismissible) banner appears in the dashboard with a **"Resend"** option — it never blocks anything.

# PART 3 — Building Your Website (the wizard)

This is the guided flow that turns a teacher's answers into a finished website. It shows a progress bar across four main stages: **Business → Setup → Templates → Design**. Everything typed is saved automatically to the browser as you go (and, for signed-in users, also to Mumotor's servers a couple of seconds after each change) so nothing is lost on a refresh. If Mumotor finds a newer saved draft from another session, it offers to **restore** it or **start fresh**.

The website's language starts out matching whatever language the teacher is using Mumotor in, and can be changed in the first step.

### Step 0 — Welcome
A friendly intro ("Let's build your driving website") with a **"Start building"** button and a note: "No design skills needed · Free to start."

### Step 1 — Business ("Tell us about you")
An **"Auto-fill sample"** button fills every field with realistic example data (in the currently chosen language) so a teacher can see how things look, then edit.

- **Business card:** Business name (required), Business description (required — teaching style, pass rate, what makes you special), and Tagline/slogan.
- **Contact card:** Phone, Email, Address, City, and the **Site language** selector (English / Hebrew / Arabic — this sets the language and reading direction of the published site).
- **Logo card:** an optional logo image (drag-and-drop or browse; up to 2 MB; PNG/JPG/SVG). If skipped, Mumotor makes a clean monogram from the business name. A preview and a "Remove" option appear once a logo is added.
- A **"Continue"** button (won't advance until a business name is entered).

### Step 2 — Setup ("Set up your lessons")
This step powers both the website content and the booking system. Five cards:

**A. Schedule & availability**
- **Working days:** seven day toggles (Mon–Sun); default Mon–Fri.
- **Shift start / Shift end** times (default 08:00–18:00).
- **"Customise hours per day"** switch — when on, each day gets its own open/close times and an open/closed checkbox.
- **Break times:** a list of start–end pairs you can add and remove (a new break defaults to 12:00–13:00).
- **"Rest between lessons"** switch — when on, set a rest gap of 5–30 minutes (default 10) inserted after each lesson.

**B. Lessons & pricing**
- **Class duration** (20/30/40/45/60/75/90 minutes; default 45).
- **Price per class** in ₪ (default 100).
- **Transmission:** Manual / Automatic / Both (default Both). The website copy and FAQ adapt to this choice.
- **Plans / packages editor:** starts with a single "Single lesson" plan seeded from your price. For each plan you set a **name**, **price**, **unit** (e.g. "/ lesson" or "10 lessons"), a **features** list (one per line), and a **"Mark as most popular"** checkbox. You can **add** more plans (e.g. a 10-lesson block) and **remove** any extra plan.

**C. Teacher profile**
- **Full name.**
- **Experience:** four cards — 1–3 / 3–5 / 5–10 / 10+ years (default 5–10).
- **Your photo** (of the instructor; up to 5 MB).
- **Car photo** (up to 5 MB).

**D. Booking rules**
- **Booking window start / end** — the daily times students may book within (default 09:00–17:00).
- **Report time** — when you finalise tomorrow's schedule and get your schedule email (default 18:00). Mumotor gently warns (without blocking) if this is set less than 30 minutes after the booking window closes.

**E. Social media & gallery**
- **Social media:** ten platform toggles — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Pinterest, Telegram, Snapchat. Turning one on reveals a link field for it.
- **Gallery photos:** an optional grid of photos to add (each up to 5 MB), each removable.

A **"Choose a design"** button moves to the gallery.

### Step 3 — Templates (pick a design)
A gallery of **all 12 designs**, each shown as a live mini-preview using your own details, with the design's name, style, and a short description. Hovering lifts a card; the selected one is highlighted. The **Mumotor** design is special: instead of a style badge it shows a row of **accent-colour dots** you can tap to recolour the whole site. Clicking any design selects it and jumps to the Design step. (The full list of all 12 designs is in the appendix at the end of this guide.)

### Step 4 — Design + live preview
- A **"Choose another template"** button to return to the gallery.
- A **"Customize"** button that opens the full-screen design editor (Part 4).
- A **"Publish my site"** button — publishes immediately if you're signed in, or asks you to create an account/sign in first.
- A **live, scrollable preview** of your chosen design filled with your real details, shown inside a browser-window mockup labelled with your business name.
- A caption reminding you to use **Customize** to change colours/text/icons/photos, or **Choose another template** to switch designs.

### Step 4a — Account (only if publishing while signed out)
A quick account step with two modes: **Register** (Full name, Email, Password) or **Login** (Email, Password). Submitting creates or signs in to the account and then immediately publishes the site.

### Step 5 — Done ("Your site is live")
A success screen showing your **live web address** with a **"Visit"** button, plus **"Go to dashboard"** and **"View live site"** buttons.

# PART 4 — The Design Editor ("Customize")

A live, full-screen visual editor where the teacher edits their actual website by **clicking directly on any part of it**. All edits are stored as a separate layer on top of the real content, so the original is never lost and everything can be undone.

### The toolbar (across the top)
- **Editing Mode** indicator (a pulsing dot) with a hint to "click any text/photo to edit."
- **Undo** and **Redo** (greyed out when unavailable; their tooltips show how many steps are available).
- **Reset** — undo *all* changes back to the design's original state.
- **Colours** — opens a panel to recolour the whole site (see below).
- **Save** — the button reads **"Save"** when there are unsaved changes and **"Saved"** when there are none. A green *"Saved"* confirmation appears at the **bottom-centre** of the screen (placed there deliberately so it never covers the Save/Done buttons).
- **Done** — leaves the editor (asks to confirm if there are unsaved edits).

### What you can edit
- **Hovering** any editable element outlines it, so you can see what's editable.
- **Clicking text** lets you type directly on the page. A small popover also offers a **text colour** and a **fill/background colour** (used for buttons and badges), each with a reset link. Empty text shows a faint *"Add text…"* hint so it's still clickable.
- **Clicking an image** opens a **Photo** panel with two options: **Upload** a file from your computer (must be an image, under 5 MB), or **Find a photo** — a built-in photo search (powered by Unsplash) pre-seeded with a smart search based on what you clicked (e.g. "driving instructor portrait" for the instructor photo). Photo credits are shown.
- **Clicking an icon** opens a searchable **icon library** grouped into helpful categories (Driving & road, Trust & achievement, Learning, People & support, Time & booking, Contact, Money & value, General). Pick any icon to swap it instantly.
- **Prices and numbers** are handled cleverly — editing a price keeps only the number.

### Colours (two ways)
- **Per-element:** the text popover's text-colour and fill controls.
- **Whole-site "Colours" panel:** each design exposes its own set of named colour "slots" (for example: Background, Text, Accent, Section bands, Muted text — the exact names differ per design), each a colour picker with a reset link. Changing one recolours the whole site.
- **Mumotor design only:** a row of **8 accent-colour dots** to set the site's single accent colour in one tap.

### Lists — add, remove, reorder
Any repeating list — **packages, FAQ questions, areas, stats, social icons, and credential chips** — can be edited. Hovering a list item shows small controls to **drag to reorder**, **add** a new item, or **delete** it. New FAQ items start with a clean blank question and answer.

### Packages always stay in sync with your plans
When you add, rename, reorder, or delete a package card in the editor and save, those changes fold straight back into your wizard **Plans** list — so the two can never disagree. (This prevents an old bug where a site could show duplicate package cards.)

### Nothing navigates while editing
In the editor, clicking a link or button (like "Book a lesson") selects it for editing instead of navigating away — so you never accidentally leave the page mid-edit.

The editor is available both from the builder's Design step and later from the dashboard (the "Customize" / "Edit site" buttons).

# PART 5 — Your Published Website (what visitors and students see)

Once published, the teacher's website lives at their own web address. Every site is one of 12 designs, all fed the exact same information, so switching designs never loses content — only the look changes. Mumotor only ever shows what the teacher actually entered; it fills genuinely empty spots with tasteful, editable starter content so a barely-filled site still looks complete, and it **never invents reviews**.

### The sections of a published site, top to bottom

**Navigation bar**
- The teacher's logo (or an auto-made monogram) and business name; clicking scrolls to the top.
- Section links: **Packages, About, Areas, Reviews, FAQ** (each smooth-scrolls). The **Reviews link only appears if there's at least one approved review.**
- A **"My account"** button (for students) that appears once the site is published.
- A main **"Book a lesson"** button.
- A mobile menu with the same links. The current section highlights as you scroll.

**Hero (the big top area)**
- A small eyebrow line (e.g. "Driving lessons in [city]").
- The headline (the teacher's tagline).
- A subtitle (the teacher's own description, or a friendly auto-written line).
- Two buttons: **Book a first lesson** and **See packages**.
- A prominent hero image (the teacher's car photo, or a gallery photo).
- On some designs, small credential chips and floating cards for polish.

**Stats band**
Up to four animated counting figures, built **only** from what the teacher entered: **years of experience**, **price per lesson (₪)**, **minutes per lesson**, and **days per week**. Nothing is invented.

**"Why learn here" cards**
A few short reassurance cards (e.g. patient one-to-one, dual-control safety, local coverage).

**Packages / Pricing**
- One card per plan the teacher created (their plans are the single source of truth — no invented offerings).
- Each card shows the **plan name**, **price with ₪**, **unit**, an optional lesson-length line, a **feature checklist**, and a **"Choose plan"** button that scrolls to booking.
- The plan marked most popular gets a **"Most popular"** badge and stands out.
- Any transmission-related feature line automatically matches the teacher's actual choice (manual / automatic / both) and the site language; custom feature text is shown exactly as written.

**About / Instructor**
- An about heading and one or two paragraphs (the teacher's description first).
- An about image.
- A reassurance checklist (one-to-one, pickup from home/work/college, dual-control insured car, honest feedback).
- The instructor block: a **prominent photo**, name, and role ("Driving instructor · 10+ years").
- Credential chips including a **transmission chip that reflects the real choice** (Manual & Automatic / Manual / Automatic).

**Areas covered**
Up to six area labels built from the teacher's city/address, plus standard entries like "Test-centre routes," "Evening & weekend slots," and "Pickup on request."

**Reviews / Testimonials**
- **Only real, approved reviews are ever shown.** If there are none, the whole section (and its nav link) is hidden.
- Each review shows a **star rating**, the **text**, the reviewer's **name**, an optional avatar, and a month-and-year line.

**Gallery**
A grid of the teacher's uploaded photos — hidden entirely if none were uploaded.

**FAQ (expandable)**
Five questions auto-written from the teacher's real details, fully translated: transmission taught, price ("Lessons are ₪X for Y minutes…"), areas covered, how quickly you can start, and the rescheduling policy.

**Booking call-to-action**
A prominent band — "Ready to start driving?" — with **Book a lesson** and **Enroll** buttons leading to the real booking and enrollment flows.

**Contact, hours, and footer**
- **Contact:** phone (tap to call), email (tap to email), and address, each with an icon.
- **Social icons**, including **WhatsApp click-to-chat** — the teacher's phone number is automatically turned into a working WhatsApp link. Instagram, Facebook, and any others follow.
- **Opening-hours table** with translated weekday names, showing each day's open–close or "Closed."
- A footer with the copyright line and a small "Built with Mumotor" credit.

### The website's own addresses
- The main site.
- An **enrollment** page for new students.
- A **book-a-lesson** page.
- A student **account** page.
- A **leave-a-review** page.

# PART 6 — The Teacher Dashboard

The private control centre a teacher uses to run everything. A left sidebar links to: **Overview, Driving Teacher (manage your school), Reviews, Messages, Publishing, Billing, Settings.** The bottom of the sidebar shows the teacher's name, email, a language switcher, and a **Sign out** button. A top bar carries the page title and a **notifications bell.** If the teacher hasn't verified their email, a gentle dismissible banner offers to resend the verification.

**The notifications bell** collects the things that need the teacher's attention: a **new student enrolled**, a **new lesson was booked**, a **student cancelled**, a **new review** arrived, and a **new chat message** came in. The teacher can open them, mark one as read, or mark all as read.

## 6.1 Overview (home)
- A **"Welcome back, [name]"** greeting.
- If the teacher has **no website yet:** a friendly card inviting them to **create one** (opens the builder).
- For **each website** the teacher owns, a card showing: the site **name** and **status** (Draft/Published), the **tagline**, the **live web address** with a **Copy link** button (shows "Copied"), a **View live site** button (when published), two stat tiles — **Students enrolled** and **Lessons booked** — and quick buttons: **Manage school**, **Edit site**, and **Enrollment link** (opens the sign-up page in a new tab).
- A **"New site"** button to build another.

## 6.2 Driving Teacher — "Manage your school"
The biggest page, with five tabs. If the teacher owns more than one site, a picker at the top chooses which school to manage.

### Tab 1 — Enrollment Code
Two cards:
- **Today's rotating code** — the day's fresh code shown large, with a **Copy code** button and a "Valid for [today's date]" note.
- **Enrollment link** — the public sign-up web address in a copyable box, with **Copy** and **Open enroll page** buttons, and a note explaining the permanent (static) code.

### Tab 2 — Students
- A **search box** (searches name/email) and a **status filter** (All / Active / Inactive / Completed).
- An **"Add student"** button opening a form: **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone** (required), and **Notes**. Mumotor checks the name, a valid email, and a valid phone before adding.
- A **table** of students showing **Name, Email, Phone, Classes** (their lesson count), **Enrolled** date, **Status**, and **Actions**. Per student you can:
  - **Pause / Activate** (toggle whether they can book),
  - **Mark completed** (they've finished their lessons),
  - **Delete** (with a confirmation that names the student).
- **Pagination** (10 students per page) when there are many.

### Tab 3 — Schedule
- A **Today / Tomorrow** toggle.
- A header line: opening hours plus "[N] booked · [M] free" (or a "closed" message).
- An **"Email me"** button that sends the teacher this day's schedule right away, and a **Refresh** button.
- A grid of **time slots**. Each is **Free** or **Booked**:
  - **Booked** slots show the **student's name**, their **phone number**, and their **lesson number**, plus a **Cancel lesson** link (which frees the slot and emails the student).
  - **Free** slots show an **"Add student"** link that opens a searchable list of active students; picking one books that student into the slot exactly as if they'd booked it themselves (so no one else can take it) — the student gets a confirmation, but the teacher does **not** get a fresh schedule email for this manual change.

### Tab 4 — Email (send a message to students)
- A **"Send to"** choice: **A group** or **Specific students**.
  - **A group:** a dropdown of All / Active / Inactive students.
  - **Specific students:** a checkbox list of active students, with a "[n] selected" count.
- A **Subject** and **Message**, and a **Send email** button. Mumotor requires a subject and message (and at least one student in "specific" mode), then reports how many were sent (and how many failed, if any).

### Tab 5 — Settings (the school's operating settings)
A long form with a sticky **Save settings** button. It contains:
- **Enrollment code card:** the permanent (static) code with a "regenerate" button (creates a clear 6-character code with no confusing letters like I/O/0/1), the copyable public enroll link, and an **"Enable daily code"** checkbox.
- **Lesson settings:** lesson duration (30/45/60/90/120 min), advance booking (how many days ahead), same-day cutoff hour, and rest-between-lessons minutes.
- **Booking window:** booking opens time, booking closes time, and the daily report time.
- **Working hours:** each weekday with an open/closed checkbox and open/close times.
- **Break times:** add/remove start–end break pairs.
- **Public profile:** teacher name, price per lesson, years of experience, and pass rate.

## 6.3 Reviews
- A list of every review with the **student name**, a **status** (pending / approved / rejected), the **star rating**, the **date**, and the review **text**.
- Per review the teacher can **Approve**, **Reject**, **Reply** (a public reply shown on the site), or **Delete**.
- Only **approved** reviews appear on the public website.
- (If the teacher owns more than one site, a picker chooses which site's reviews to view.)

## 6.4 Messages (chat with students)
- A list of student conversations, each with the student's initial, name, a **last-message preview**, and an **unread count** badge.
- Opening a conversation shows the full thread (teacher messages on one side, student on the other, with timestamps) and a **reply box**.
- The list refreshes about every 12 seconds and an open thread about every 8 seconds, so new messages appear on their own. Opening a thread marks its messages read.

## 6.5 Publishing
- A row per website with its **name**, **status**, and **web address** (with a copy button).
- Buttons to **Customize** (open the editor), **Visit** (when live), and to **Publish** or **Unpublish** the site.
- Each time a site is published, Mumotor quietly keeps a **saved snapshot (version)** of it, so there's always a history of what was published.
- **Deleting** a site is permanent and removes everything belonging to it (its students, bookings, reviews, messages, photos, codes, and version history) — which is why it requires typing the word DELETE to confirm (in the account Settings danger zone).

## 6.6 Billing
- Shows the current plan and the available plan(s) as cards, each with an icon, price, feature list, and a button to switch. The real plan is **PRO — ₪199/month.** (In the live product, upgrading goes through secure Stripe checkout; a demo note explains test mode.)

## 6.7 Settings (the teacher's account)
- **Profile:** Full name, Email (shown but not editable here), Phone, and **Preferred language** (English/Hebrew/Arabic — changing it immediately switches the dashboard language). A **Save profile** button.
- **Change password:** current password + new password (at least 8 characters).
- **Danger zone — Delete a website:** a list of owned sites, each with a **Delete** button that opens a confirmation requiring the teacher to **type the word DELETE** before the permanent-delete button activates.

# PART 7 — The Student Experience

Students use pages on the teacher's own website. Everything is themed to match the teacher's chosen design and is shown in the teacher's chosen language (with numbers kept as standard digits). Every student page ends with a small "Powered by Mumotor" footer. If a link is wrong or a site isn't published, a friendly "School not found" message appears.

## 7.1 Enroll (join a school)
The first-time sign-up page. A student enters:
- **Full name**, **Email**, **Phone** (required), and the **Enrollment code** their instructor gave them (the code box automatically shows letters in uppercase for clarity).
- An **"Enroll"** button. Mumotor checks the name, a valid phone, and a long-enough code before submitting.
- A note: *"Already enrolled? Book a lesson."*
- On success, a welcome screen with buttons to **Book a lesson** or **Go to my account.** If the email is already registered, it says so kindly and offers the same buttons. A welcome email is sent.

## 7.2 Book a lesson
A short step-by-step flow. **Lessons are booked for tomorrow only.**
- **Email step:** enter the email you enrolled with, then **Continue**. If the teacher set a daily booking window, it's shown ("Booking is open daily 09:00–17:00 (Israel time)"). *This step is skipped automatically if the student is already signed in to their account on this site* (the booking page also technically supports a one-time email "magic link," a capability that is built-in but not currently sent anywhere — see "Built and ready, but not switched on" near the end).
  - If the email isn't recognised, a quick **enroll step** appears (name, phone, code) so they can join and continue.
  - If the enrollment is paused, they're told to contact their instructor.
- **Choose a time:** shows tomorrow's date, the lesson length, and an "arrive 5 minutes early" note, then a grid of available time slots shown as ranges (e.g. **08:00 – 08:45**). Helpful messages cover the cases where booking is closed right now, the school is closed tomorrow, or every slot is already taken.
- **Confirm:** a summary of the date and time with **Change time** and **Confirm booking** buttons. Two students can never grab the same slot — if someone just took it, the student is asked to pick again.
- **Done:** "Lesson booked," the date and time, a reminder note ("We'll send you a reminder before your lesson"), an "arrive 5 minutes early" note, and buttons to **Book another lesson**, **Go to my account**, or **Back to home.**
- There is deliberately **no cancellation option and no cancellation-time wording** anywhere in booking. A confirmation email is sent.

## 7.3 Student account
A simple personal area. Returning students **sign in with just their email — no password and no code.**
Three tabs:
- **Lessons:** a highlighted **next lesson** (date, time, "arrive 5 minutes early," and "to change or cancel a lesson, contact your instructor"), real statistics (**Completed / Upcoming / Total**), a list of any **later lessons**, and a **Book a lesson** button. **Students cannot cancel or reschedule here** — they're told to contact the instructor.
- **Chat:** a two-way conversation with the instructor. Messages appear as bubbles with timestamps; the tab shows an **unread count**; new messages arrive on their own (the thread refreshes every few seconds).
- **Profile:** name and email (shown, not editable) and an **editable phone** with a note that it's the number the instructor uses to reach them; a **Save changes** button (active only after an edit).
A **Sign out** button is in the header.

## 7.4 Leave a review
- Fields: **Your name**, a **five-star rating** (default 5), and **Your review** text.
- A **Send review** button.
- On success: a thank-you screen explaining the review will appear on the site **once the teacher approves it.** (Reviews are always moderated.)

# PART 8 — Emails Mumotor Sends Automatically

All student-facing emails are **branded as the teacher's own school** (the school's name/logo in the header, the school name in the subject and footer), with a small "powered by Mumotor" credit. Account emails (password reset, email verification) are plain Mumotor-branded.

1. **Welcome / enrollment email** — the moment a student enrolls (or the teacher adds them), explaining they'll get a morning email when booking opens.
2. **Booking confirmation** — instantly when a lesson is booked (by the student or by the teacher), with date, time, duration, instructor, and "arrive 5 minutes early."
3. **Lesson reminder** — automatically before each lesson.
4. **Cancellation email** — when a lesson is cancelled. If the *teacher* cancels, the student is told (apologetically). If the *student* cancels (only possible earlier than the last two hours), the *teacher* is told the slot reopened.
5. **"Booking is now open" email** — each morning at the teacher's booking-window-open time, to every active student, inviting them to book tomorrow.
6. **The teacher's daily schedule report** — once a day at the teacher's report time. A polished, Apple-style layout: three tiles (Booked / Free / Total), then each slot in time order with the student's name and a **tappable phone number** to call or message them. It can also be sent on demand from the Schedule tab.
7. **Bulk email** — whatever the teacher writes, personalised with each student's name, to the chosen audience.
8. **One-time booking link ("magic link")** — a built-in capability to email a returning student a one-time link that lets them book without re-entering their code (works once, expires in 15 minutes). *Note: this capability is present in the system but is not currently triggered anywhere in the live product — see "Built and ready, but not switched on."*
9. **Review request** — automatically about an hour after a lesson ends, inviting a review.
10. **Password reset** — a one-time link (30-minute expiry).
11. **Email verification** — on sign-up and on "resend" (24-hour expiry).

Sending an email never blocks or breaks anything — if delivery fails, sign-up, enrollment, and booking still succeed.

# PART 9 — Things That Happen Automatically on a Schedule

Mumotor runs quiet background routines so the teacher doesn't have to:

- **Every 15 minutes:** it sends **lesson reminders** for lessons starting soon, and sends **review requests** for lessons that finished about an hour ago. Each is sent only once.
- **Every 5 minutes** (the "daily rhythm"): for each published school, comparing the current time in Israel against the teacher's own settings —
  - at the teacher's **booking-open time**, it makes sure today's access code exists and emails all active students that booking is open for tomorrow;
  - at the teacher's **report time**, it emails the teacher tomorrow's full schedule.
  - These fire **once per day** each, and are **self-healing**: if the server was briefly down at the exact minute, the next run still catches up and sends exactly once.

# PART 10 — The Rules That Keep Everything Correct

These are the invisible rules that make the product trustworthy.

**Booking rules**
- Booking is for **tomorrow only** (this is the default "advance booking" of one day).
- Booking is only allowed **within the teacher's daily booking window.**
- You can't book a time in the past, and same-day booking closes after the teacher's cutoff hour.
- The chosen time must be a **real available slot** for that day.
- Only an **enrolled, active** student can book.

**How available slots are worked out**
Mumotor walks through the day from the open time to the close time in steps of the lesson length (plus any rest gap), skipping times that are already booked or that fall inside a break, and only offering a slot if the full lesson fits before closing.

**No double-bookings — guaranteed**
Two students can never book the same slot. Even if they tap "confirm" at the exact same instant, the system guarantees only one succeeds; the other is politely asked to pick again. A cancelled slot becomes bookable again.

**Enrollment codes and student identity**
- New students join with either the teacher's **permanent code** or the **daily rotating code.**
- Codes are never stored in plain text.
- **Returning students sign in with just their email** — the code is only the gate for new students.
- A paused student can't book and is told to contact the instructor.

**Students can't cancel late**
Students cannot cancel within two hours of a lesson — they're directed to contact their instructor. (In the current product, the student pages don't expose a self-cancel button at all; teachers can always cancel.)

**Privacy and safety (in plain terms)**
- Each teacher's data — students, bookings, reviews, messages, photos — is completely separate from every other teacher's; a teacher can only ever see and manage their own.
- Teachers and students have entirely separate sign-ins; neither can act as the other, and a student's sign-in only works on the school it belongs to.
- **A phone number is required** wherever an identity is created (teacher sign-up, student enroll, teacher-adds-student, student profile) because the teacher needs it for lesson coordination and the daily report.
- Sensitive actions (sign-in, sign-up, enrolling, booking, password reset, sending bulk email, uploading photos) are rate-limited to prevent abuse.
- "Forgot password," "resend verification," and "request booking link" never reveal whether an email exists, to protect privacy.
- Reset links, verification links, and one-time booking links each truly work only once and then expire.
- Uploaded images are checked to be genuine image files (not just by their name) and are size-limited.

# PART 14 — Built and Ready, but Not Currently Switched On

For completeness, these capabilities exist fully in Mumotor's code but are **not active in the live product today**. They're listed here so nothing is hidden:

- **Per-instructor custom web addresses** (like `yourname.mumotor.com`): fully built, currently switched off; today every site lives at a Mumotor sub-path instead.
- **The one-time booking "magic link" email:** the system can email a returning student a one-time booking link (and the booking page can accept it), but nothing in the live product currently sends one — the student account (email-only sign-in) covers this need instead.
- **Older student self-service by email + code:** the system still contains earlier routes for a student to look up their own bookings, cancel by email + code, or pause their own enrollment. These were replaced by the newer email-only student account (where students can view lessons and chat, but cannot cancel — they contact the instructor), so they're no longer offered anywhere in the current pages.
- **The original built-in HTML site generator:** an earlier engine that produced a site's page as fixed HTML from a set of presets still exists behind the scenes, but the live websites students actually see are the newer, richer template designs described in Part 5.
